本論文旨在探討異質整合檢索系統間運用之技術及功能，以為圖書館採購產品之參考依 　據；並進一步瞭解目前國內已使用異質整合檢索系統之單位現況及具體成效。為達前述目 　的，本論文以MetaFind、SmartWeaver、HySearch、MuseSearch及MetaLib/ SFX 為例，運 　用文獻分析法、訪談法及實証研究法，探討異質整合檢索系統相關議題文獻，擬定訪談大 　綱，並產出系統評估準則以實際於系統中驗證之。

The WebFeat Prism search engine and translator system will enable users to conduct true federated searching at their library. Users will be able to search ISI Web of Knowledge resources and their library's electronic databases at the same time through a single interface. What’s more, librarians will have the capability to customize the WebFeat Prism search interface to their preferences and their patrons' needs; specify access; and easily organize and manage all electronic resources in their library.
