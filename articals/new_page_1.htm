Professional development is different from continue education.

Professional development usually refers to some activities in specific field.  Any activities that can help you to develop your profession and expertise can count.  For example, professional conferences, workshops, internship (for students)….

 

Continue education can be the activities that are not necessary relevant to your profession.

 

So, I guess then you can tell what is different with career development and professional development.

I think there is no solid definition to compare. However, one might define them differently for different purpose.

In my opinion, professional development can be a subset of career development, because I feel that career development is more like ‘life plan’.
