On the Development of Hypermedia Dissertations
and Thesis on TANET


Chang Yin-Yih*

Abstract 

    Dissertations(Ph. D.) and thesis(M.S.) are not only the results

of research, but also the essentials to researchers. They are

traditionally paper-based for researchers to read. Some dissertations

and thesis are transformed into computer files, but can only be fetch

for printing. Some others support on-line query, but provide only 

titles and abstracts. The above dissertations and thesis do not have

capabilities, such as, interactive reading, search and copy. 
    Since dissertations and thesis are essentials to research, there

is a need for circulation on the network. In this paper, we utilize

the world-wide-web standards, communication protoclos, the high-

bandwidth of TANET and multimedia capabilities of computers; we  

analyze the documentation structure of dissertations and thesis with  

hypertext and hypermedia technology; and finally proposed a framework  

of hypermedia dissertations and thesis on TANET. 
    In this research project, we have the following contributions; 
    1.Proposed a construction method of hypermedia dissertations and

thesis server for all universities on TANET. 
    2.With the dissertations and thesis published by the National

Taiwan Univeersity as a starting point, developed a prototype of

hypermedia dissertations and thesis server, and had a successful

experiment test on TANET. 
    Given the proposed technology, we had made possiblee that the

circulation over network of journals and querying over TANET. 
Keyword:Electronic Dissertations and Thesis, Taiwan Academic Network

        (TANET),World Wide Web(WWW). 
*Department of Industrial Management, Chang Gung College of Medicine

 and Technology)
