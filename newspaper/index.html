聯合線上「全版報紙資料庫」 2009.7.15 
聯合線上推出的第二代電子報紙「全版報紙資料庫」，同時保有數位媒體的特色，例如每天與紙本報紙同時出刊、遠距同步取得出版內容，並且善用資料庫功能，可以貯存大量（一年報紙）資料提供瀏覽、逐筆查詢新聞並呈現全版內容、依個人需求暫存線上報夾。在資訊完全超載的現代環境，「全版報紙資料庫」提供精確完整的報紙形式新聞，可大幅節省使用者漫遊在新聞與雜訊的時間，更可滿足金融商界、媒體公關、檢調律師、研究人員等專業人士蒐查佐證資料的需求。對於海外台商，全版報紙資料庫更是足抵萬金的鄉情商訊。 聯合線上推出的「全版報紙資料庫」，將以經濟日報和聯合報為服務核心，數位化加值工具也將陸續增加，並準備迎接後紙張─電子紙媒體世代的到來。

民國三十四年創刊的《民報》，在三十六年二月廿八日「二二八事件」發生當日停刊。雖然發行只有十七個月，《民報》反映了光復之後到二二八事件發生前的台灣社會實況，因此成為台灣史研究的重要依據。國立台中圖書館推動舊版報紙數位化，首先將《民報》完整掃描，在網路上開放民眾檢索使用。「舊版報紙資訊網」計畫為文建會「國家文化資料庫」建構計畫的一部分，將選擇具有史料價值、報社已經不存在，且有完整收集的舊版報紙做為數位化對象。文建會副主委吳密察說，國立台中圖書館成立於民國十二年，收藏許多日治時期的報紙、期刊和圖書。由於這些文獻的數量有限，經過幾十年保存，紙質已經得十分脆弱，基於「文化民主化」理念、為了讓民眾可以自由參考使用，唯有借用數位科技，將典藏的紙本資料做成資料庫，開放在網路上運用。國立台中圖書館以掃描方式全版儲存《民報》，另外建立新聞標題和廣告索引，可以透過關鍵字或是日期的查詢檢索，找到相關的全版影像。除了《民報》數位化，國立台中圖書館也將陸續進行《公論報》（民國41年3月至民國52年10月）、《民聲報》（民國41年1月至74年8月）的數位化作業。

National Digital Newspaper Program (NDNP)2005.06.03
A partnership between the Library of Congress and the National Endowment for the Humanities
The Library of Congress is pleased to announce a new Web site, providing an overview and technical specifications for the development phase of the National Digital Newspaper Program (NDNP). This program, a partnership between the National Endowment of the Humanities (NEH) and the Library of Congress (LC), is a long-term effort to develop an Internet-based, searchable database of all U.S. newspapers with descriptive information and digitization of select historic pages. Supported by NEH, this rich digital resource will be developed and permanently maintained at the Library of Congress. An NEH grant program will fund the contribution of content from, eventually, all U.S. states and territories.
An initial development phase will run through 2007, and will include content from 6 NEH state awardees (University of California, Riverside; University of Florida Libraries, Gainesville; University of Kentucky Libraries, Lexington; New York Public Library, New York City; University of Utah, Salt Lake City; and Library of Virginia, Richmond) providing 100,000 pages each of historic material published between 1900-1910. In addition, the Library of Congress will contribute 100,000 pages from its own historic collections, representing the District of Columbia.
Program information for the National Digital Newspaper Program is available from the Library of Congress's Preservation Web site: http://www.loc.gov/preserv  .

The Japan News Digital Archive(讀賣新聞英文日報資料庫)
讀賣新聞英文日報資料庫收錄《讀賣新聞》英文報Japan News，於1955年創刊並持續發行迄今，是世界最大的新聞傳播網絡之一，見證了戰後日本快速變遷的社會，同時也陪伴讀者走過昭和、平成、令和新時代。本庫共33萬5千200頁，除了透過標題與日期的檢索方式外，亦隨機選擇歷史上的今天供讀者檢閱，是研究日本與東亞的重要資源。
The Newspaper Reading Room
The Newspaper Reading Room provides access to the British Library's collection of British and overseas newspapers. The collection, which also includes popular magazines and periodicals, is made available in hard copy, in microform, and on CD-ROM or online format.
Tímarit
Timarit.is是供使用紙本文化遺產的數位圖書館，目的在透過新的資訊技術提高紙本報紙和期刊的使用，保存了Faroe Islands, Greenland and Iceland之報紙和期刊，資源內容包含文學，歷史，政治，商業，貿易和工業等不同學科的一般報紙和廣告。
Timarit.is is a digital library where millions of pages in digital format are made available on the Internet. This gives access to the printed cultural heritage that is preserved in newspapers and periodicals of the Faroe Islands, Greenland and Iceland. The access is open to everyone and the material is made available by using the latest methods in information technology. The goal is to enhance access to printed newspapers and periodicals and to enable new methods for researchers. In addition to general news and advertisements the newspapers and periodicals contain and refer to many diverse subjects like literature, history, politics, business, trade and industry. Users can search the database both by using fixed terms title and country, and by using full text search. They can also browse through the material and print selected pages. The digital library will steadily accumulate more titles from the participating countries

Trove (Digitised Newspapers and more )
Find and get over 255,685,876 Australian and online resources: books, images, historic newspapers, maps, music, archives and more
Trove is a discovery experience focused on Australia and Australians. It supplements what search engines provide. If you are researching in the fields of the social sciences, literature, local or family history, or need inspiration for your school assignment, then this is the tool for you. For example if researching images relating to Edmund Barton, our first Prime Minister, results will include descriptions such as people, book, manuscript, map and newspaper articles. A researcher searching for information on Nellie Melba will be presented with a range of results including biographies, pictures, music, newspapers, books etc.
